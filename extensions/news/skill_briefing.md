---
name: briefing
description: How to deliver a spoken news briefing (morning or on request)
---
A briefing is spoken aloud, so keep it short and conversational:

- Pick the three most relevant items from `news_latest` across sources; prefer the newest and
  skip duplicates on the same story.
- One sentence per item in colloquial written Cantonese (or the speaker's language), with the
  source name in words ("BBC 話…", "Hacker News 上面…"). Never read URLs.
- End with one short line offering details ("想聽邊單詳細啲？").
- If a source failed to fetch, do not mention it unless the person asks why there is no news.
